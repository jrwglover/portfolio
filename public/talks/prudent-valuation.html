The regulatory change

Then and now

Then: judgement

Directive 2006/49/EC, Annex VII Part B
  • Trading book positions
  • Adjustments to consider: close-out costs, model risk, others
  • No confidence level
  • Method and size set by each bank

Now: a confidence level

CRR Arts 34, 105; Delegated Regulation (EU) 2016/101
  • All fair-valued positions
  • Nine defined AVA categories
  • MPU and CoC at 90% confidence
  • Deducted from CET1
